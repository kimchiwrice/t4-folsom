Our Story

Your Spot
in Folsom

T4 Folsom is a woman-owned boba shop tucked inside Broadstone Plaza. We're part of the T4 family — a Taiwanese tea brand that's been around since 2004 — and we're proud to bring that to Folsom.

We keep things simple: friendly service, quick wait times, and a comfortable spot to hang out. Whether you're grabbing a milk tea on the go or sitting down with friends, we want you to feel welcome every time you walk in.

Our tea is made with leaves sourced from T4's farms in Taiwan, and we use liquid sugar with 25% fewer calories than regular sugar. You can customize everything — sweetness, ice level, and toppings — so it's always made the way you like it.
